Cross-border

Mozambique and the SADC region, by road

RoadMaster crosses the Ngwenya border post for business travel, conferences and group trips into Mozambique and neighbouring SADC countries.

Route

Regular crossings at Ngwenya

Our vehicles regularly cross into South Africa and on to Mozambique via the Ngwenya border post, one of Eswatini's main routes into the SADC road network. Trips range from same-day return runs to multi-day business travel.

Because border-crossing trips vary widely in distance, they're priced differently from local hire — see the exact bands on the Pricing page.
