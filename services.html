Services

Six ways to book a RoadMaster vehicle

Every trip starts the same way — tell us the route and dates — but the vehicle and pricing depend on what you're moving and how far it's going.

01

Taxi

Sedan transport for individual and small trips — airport runs, town errands, and point-to-point travel around Manzini and beyond. This is the fastest vehicle class to book for a single passenger or a small group of up to four.

Book a taxi
02

7-Seater

Our VW 7-seaters cover family travel, small group transport, and conference or business trip transfers where a sedan is too small. They're also the vehicle used for most of our cross-border runs.

Book a 7-seater
03

Truck

Mini trucks for furniture removals, goods transport and general freight. Because load size and distance vary so much, truck jobs are priced with a direct quote based on your destination rather than a flat day rate.

Request a truck quote
04

Business trips

Companies book RoadMaster for staff travel between towns, site visits, and airport transfers. Email is typically the preferred communication channel for business bookings, since it keeps a written quotation and confirmation on record.

Book business travel
05

Conference & group transport

For delegations and group bookings, we can coordinate several vehicles from our fleet on the same route and schedule, with one point of contact for the whole group's booking.

Enquire for a group
06

Cross-border transport

Regular trips into Mozambique and the wider SADC region, for both individual travellers and business delegations. See the Cross-Border page for route details and the Pricing page for distance-based fees.

See cross-border details
